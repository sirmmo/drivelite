# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project and there is no reason for
anyone's day to get worse because of it.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings about context.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time, and that "no" is a legitimate
  answer to a feature request.
- Respect the scope of the project, even when you disagree with its limits.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about who someone is — including
  their gender, sexuality, disability, appearance, ethnicity, age, or beliefs.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad faith argument.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and
commit history — and to public behaviour where someone is representing the
project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to **marco.montanari@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled confidentially, and I will not discuss a report with anyone who is not
directly involved in resolving it.

Depending on severity, the response may be a private word, a request to edit
or withdraw a comment, or a block from the project. Maintainers who do not
act in good faith on these principles should be held to them too.
